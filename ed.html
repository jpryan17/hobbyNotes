Middle way mathematics is an approach to mathematics sufficient to support a general understanding of the mathematics that underlies quantum statistical mechanics. This understanding might enhance one's general scientific literacy. This understanding will not enhance one's technical expertise in this field.

Even a modest model of the mathematics of quantum statistical mechanics will be relatively complex. For most, some effort is involved in absorbing all the required ideas, even if each idea is simple enough. It is a relatively long journey. It takes time. So middle way mathematics is not described in one chunk.

It is convenient to present middle way mathematics via a series of lectures that span a curricular arc, starting at the primary level with propositional logic, and continuing through to university level lectures where analysis and probability are addressed.
