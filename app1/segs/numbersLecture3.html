But to define operators, we must also know some general facts about how sets are classified according to their size, or more formally, cardinality. Without going into detail, we will recognize three classes of set size
  • finite, countable, and uncountable.
A finite set is considered countable; a countable set is a set that can be placed in a 1 to 1 correspondence with the natural numbers (or in the finite case, a subset of the natural numbers), and as such, may be enumerated and counted. An uncountable set has too many members to be enumerated and counted; for example both ℝωℝ_ω and ℂωℂ_ω are uncountable.
