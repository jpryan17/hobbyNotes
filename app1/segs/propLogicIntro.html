propositional logic

As mentioned in the page introduction, a primary goal of propositional logic is to expose the student to formal science in general and Boolean logic in particular on familiar ground.

A rather stilted teacher/classroom interaction is used to introduce the teacher to a perspective of propositional logic, excuse me, a perspective of the formalization of propositional logic appropriate for this level of education: as a game that only cares about being right.

The pedagogic key to presentation is the truth table demo, which gives immediate visual confirmation of an expression's value for any given input.
