The idea was suggested in an LLM session that variables quantified predicates could be translated into Boolean matrices that could be displayed on a grid that, in it's limit, is the transfinite grid determined by ℂωℂ_ω in the case of a binary predicate.

In general, after successfully constructing a quantified predicate, it's resulting truth structure could be displayed and examined by

  • first displaying the values of quantified predicates as one displays a row in a truth table
  • and clicking on a predicate will display the truth state in terms of Boolean matrices.
Well, that should get us started.
