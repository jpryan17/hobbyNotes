We shall face the dreaded backward E and the upside down A, those symbols for the highbrow phrases 'there exists' and 'for all". But, tradition and all, we need to get used to them. So let's decode: see what's being said.
  • ∃∃ means "there exists" which means that there is some member of the of the predicate domain that evaluates to or assigns the value true;
  • ∀∀ means "for all", which means that every  member of the domain that evaluates to true.
