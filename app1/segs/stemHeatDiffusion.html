1. The 1D Heat Rod & The Discrete Mesh on ℝ_ω

Consider a conductive metal rod of length L = 1.0 partitioned into N discrete cells with uniform lattice spacing Δx = 1/(N-1) on the hyperfinite transect ℝ_ω:

Node:     [ 0 ]  ───Δx───>  [ i - 1 ]  <── flux ──> [ i ]  <── flux ──> [ i + 1 ]  ───Δx───>  [ N ]
Temp:     u₀(t)               u_{i-1}                     u_i                     u_{i+1}               u_N(t)

By Fourier’s Law of Thermal Conduction, the conductive heat flux flowing across the boundary from cell i-1 to cell i is proportional to the discrete difference quotient:

q_{i - 1/2} = -k · [ u_i - u_{i-1} ] / Δx

The net rate of heat energy accumulation at node i is the incoming flux minus the outgoing flux:

du_i / dt = α · [ u_{i-1} - 2u_i + u_{i+1} ] / Δx²

where α = k / (ρ · c) is the thermal diffusivity of the material.

2. The Tridiagonal Discrete Laplacian: NEAR (≈) and EQ (=)

Jill raised an eyebrow, looking at the matrix on the screen: “Wait, Professor James—should I already know what a 'tridiagonal Toeplitz' matrix is? It sounds like heavy academic jargon. Why on earth is it relevant to heating a steel rod?”

Professor James laughed with approval: “Never let 19th-century terminology intimidate you, Jill! That name actually describes two simple physical properties you already understand intuitively:”

  1. What 'Toeplitz' means — Spatial Homogeneity:
    Notice how every descending diagonal has the exact same constant value (all -2 on the main diagonal, all +1 on the bands directly above and below). In mathematics, any matrix where A_{i,j} = a_{i-j} is called Toeplitz. In physics, this simply means the material is uniform everywhere! A steel rod has the exact same thermal conductivity whether you measure at node 3, node 7, or node 14. Space is homogeneous—the laws of heat conduction don't change just because you slide along the rod.

  2. What 'Tridiagonal' means — Locality (The NEAR Relation):
    There are only three non-zero diagonals: the center line and its two immediate neighbors. Everything else is zero! Why? Because heat cannot jump through telepathy. Node i only touches node i-1 and node i+1. This is the exact physical embodiment of our Middle Way relations:
    • The main diagonal (-2) is the identity relation EQ (=): self-dissipation as heat leaves cell i.
    • The off-diagonal bands (+1) exist strictly where NEAR(x₁, x₂) (≈) holds: conductive exchange across the immediate boundary.
    • All other entries are 0 because distant nodes (|x₁ - x₂| > 1) have no direct contact without passing through intermediate cells!

“So,” Jill summarized with a smile, “'tridiagonal Toeplitz' is just formal math-speak for local physics on a uniform rod!”

“Precisely!” James beamed. “And what makes it so powerful for computational engineering is that because it is tridiagonal and Toeplitz, Maxima can derive its eigensystem symbolically in closed form without guessing. Its eigenvectors are none other than pure Fourier sine waves!”

5. The Fundamental Conservation Law: Machine-Verified in Lean 4

While individual temperatures drop and flatten, what happens to the total thermal energy across the rod?

Under insulated boundary conditions (zero flux at the rod ends: q₀ = q_N = 0), the rate of total energy change is:

d/dt [ ∑_{i=1}^{N} u_i(t) · Δx ] = ∑_{i=1}^{N} [ q_{i - 1/2} - q_{i + 1/2} ]

Notice the right-hand side: it is a telescoping sum! Every internal boundary term cancels exactly:

(q_{1/2} - q_{3/2}) + (q_{3/2} - q_{5/2}) + ... + (q_{N - 1/2} - q_{N + 1/2}) = q_{1/2} - q_{N + 1/2} = 0
