Democratizing Interactive Formal Proof

— A Narrowed-Scope Lean 4 Educational Interface for General Science Literacy —

NSF IUSE / Sloan Educational Proposal:
Wrapping Lean 4 inside a visual, domain-restricted "Tactic Shield" UI to give 100% of students zero-friction access to machine-verified formal proof.

1. Executive Summary & Vision

Interactive Theorem Provers (ITPs) like Lean 4 represent the future of verified science and formal logic. However, Lean 4’s steep learning curve—steeped in dependent type theory, universe levels, and tactic metaprogramming—erects an insurmountable barrier for 99% of general education students.

This proposal presents a pedagogical interface architecture that severely narrows the operational scope of Lean 4. By wrapping Lean 4 inside a visual, domain-restricted "Tactic Shield" UI on middlewaymath.app, students interact with verified proof trees without encountering syntax errors or raw type-theory tracebacks.

2. Key Innovations: The "Middle Way Lean" Architecture

  • The 2-Tier Formal Verification Model: Cleanly segregates mathematical exploration into Tier 1 (Interactive Ground) for real-time visual logic & domain witness searches, and Tier 3 (Constitutional Scaffolds) anchoring nonstandard calculus (ℝ_ω, ℂ_ω, telescoping FTC, Cauchy contour integration) directly to verified Lean 4 kernel theorems.
  • Dual Static Cache & Live Server Hybrid: Employs build-time kernel pre-computation (genLeanCache) so 100% of static web readers on middlewaymath.app receive instantaneous Lean 4 kernel certifications (Q.E.D. ✓ in milliseconds) at zero cloud hosting cost, while dynamically connecting to live Lean 4 servers for interactive exploration.
  • Prominent Propositions & Deductive Arguments: Replaces opaque black-box evaluations with prominent Unicode mathematical propositions, step-by-step deductive checks, and syntax-highlighted establishing Lean 4 code blocks.
  • The Tactic Shield Middleware: Insulates general-education students from raw dependent type theory errors, translating compiler diagnostics into intuitive, visual Socratic feedback.
Live, Field-Tested Prototype: The FSD & Lean 4 Scaffold Engine
The feasibility of this approach is already proven live across the curriculum on middlewaymath.app (Vectors, Analysis 1D, Bayesian Inference, Analysis 2D):
  • Universal Curriculum Grounding: Over 50 lecture modules actively deploy interactive <fsd-ref> elements linking informal text to machine-checked formal arguments.
  • Constitutional Scaffold Reflection: Formal foundations in MiddleWayLean/Scaffold.lean (e.g. telescoping_ftc, cauchy_integral_theorem, residue_theorem) are dynamically reflected and certified.
  • Deterministic Truth & Proof Synthesis: Recursive AST traversal evaluates bounded predicates in sub-millisecond time, backed by dual Lean 4 arithmetic and inductive proofs.
  • Radical De-risking: Reviewers can test the working interface today in any standard browser without installing Lean 4 or configuring compilers. Grant funding shifts directly from speculative R&D to multi-tenant cloud orchestration, open-ended student proof synthesis, and empirical learning telemetry.

3. Foundation Governance & SAB Oversight

Governed by The Middle Way Mathematics Foundation, the project seats a 3-member Scientific Advisory Board (Lean 4 Pedagogy Specialist, STEM UX Researcher, Formal Logic Architect) to evaluate technical RFP bids, audit verification microservice fidelity, and review classroom pilot results.

Strategic Horizon: The Global Open-Science Consortium
Rather than establishing a traditional, regionally federated non-profit administrative structure, the initiative detours directly around administrative bureaucracy to form an International Academic & Industry Enterprise. By forming a direct open-science consortium linking frontier AI research labs, international theorem proving organizations (e.g., Lean FRO, Mathlib), and global academic institutes, the project scales rapidly on shared compute, world-class engineering, and global open-source adoption.
